The first analysis

Leonhard Thurneysser carries out the first recorded alchemical analysis of the water and publishes it in his book Pison.

ROI is founded

Inspired by the spring's centuries-old history and the legend of Pegasus, still the emblem on every bottle.

The miracle of Zrinski

Count Petar Zrinski, suffering from jaundice, drinks from a hollow willow trunk at the spring. He recovers and carries word of the water to Europe's royal courts.

The God Potion

Dr Paul de Sorbait, physician to Emperor Leopold I, calls it Göttertrank in Praxis Medicae. Vienna's doctors begin prescribing it.

Roitschocrene

Joannes Benedict Gründel writes the first monograph on the water, the only one of its kind in Europe. Its title still sits on every bottle.

An imperial seal

Empress Maria Theresa decrees that every bottle carry a dated label and a symbol changed each year, to stop counterfeits.

The spa resort

Count Ferdinand Attems acquires the land around the springs. Rogaška spa resort is born.

Best in the world

At the Chicago World's Fair the water wins the medal for the best quality bottled water in the world.
